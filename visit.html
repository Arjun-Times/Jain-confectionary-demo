Questions people ask

Which areas do you deliver to?

Anywhere within 1 km of the shop is free on orders from ₹300. Farther than that, send your order and we’ll tell you the delivery charge on WhatsApp before we leave.

How do I pay?

Cash or UPI when your order arrives. You don’t pay anything on the website.

Can I send my handwritten list?

Yes. Type it on the home page, or send a photo of it on WhatsApp. We’ll confirm the total before packing.

What if something I want isn’t on the website?

The shop stocks much more than the website shows. Add it in the “Anything not on the website?” box at checkout, or ask on WhatsApp and we’ll check the shelf.

Can I get my monthly ration delivered on a fixed date?

Yes. Use the Bulk orders page to send your monthly list and the date, and we’ll deliver it together.
